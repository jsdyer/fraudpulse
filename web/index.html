Data, privacy & attribution

Filtering and CSV exports happen in this browser. The app downloads bundled public datasets; filter choices are included in the page URL for sharing and may appear in hosting logs. No report text, personal information or uploads are collected. CoolCode’s shared counter records aggregate tool opens, excluding URL parameters. Government data retains its original licence and limitations. Source links and snapshot dates appear in Sources & freshness.
